# Project Vision

## North star

Build a long running evidence system that converts heterogeneous public observations into transparent social indicators and dated probabilistic forecasts, then measures those forecasts against reality.

The product should become more useful as history accumulates. Historical continuity, falsifiability, and calibration matter more than short term novelty.

## Primary users

The initial user is the project owner. The architecture should leave a path toward a public or commercial product without requiring an early multi user platform.

## Questions the product should eventually answer

* What important changes are occurring in the world now?
* Which changes appear temporary and which appear persistent?
* Which emerging trends deserve attention before they are widely recognized?
* What is the probability of a specified major event within a specified time horizon?
* Which observations and indicators contributed to that probability?
* How accurate have previous forecasts been?
* Which forecast methods, data families, and models are well calibrated?
* What evidence is relevant to investment research and long horizon personal planning?

## Product boundaries

Psychohistory is an evidence and forecasting system. It must communicate uncertainty and preserve the distinction between observation, inference, forecast, and decision.

The project should not claim deterministic prediction of history. It should not present media attention as direct measurement of real world severity. It should not rewrite prior forecasts after outcomes become known.

## Strategic product layers

### Observation layer

Collect multiple public data families. Candidate families may include news and event data, macroeconomic data, financial market data, conflict data, energy data, public health data, demographic data, climate and disaster data, technology adoption signals, and other measurable social signals.

Specific providers remain subject to research and validation.

### Indicator layer

Transform observations into transparent indicators with documented units, direction, update frequency, geographic scope, normalization method, and known limitations.

### State and trend layer

Estimate changes in broader social conditions from multiple indicators. Composite indices should remain explainable and decomposable.

### Forecast layer

Support two forecast classes:

* Macro trend forecasts, such as whether a condition will strengthen or weaken over a defined horizon.
* Concrete event forecasts, with a clear event definition, time horizon, probability, resolution source, and resolution rule.

### Evaluation layer

Measure calibration, discrimination, error, stability, and model performance through time. Forecast methodology should improve from recorded evidence rather than retrospective narrative.

### Decision support layer

Expose relevant evidence for investment research and personal planning while avoiding unsupported prescriptions. Decision support should show assumptions, uncertainty, scenario sensitivity, and source quality.

## Success criteria

The project becomes meaningfully successful when it can demonstrate all of the following over a sustained period:

1. Reliable automated data acquisition with visible failure states.
2. Reproducible indicators with historical continuity.
3. Forecast records that are timestamped and immutable.
4. Objective resolution rules for a meaningful set of forecasts.
5. Quantitative evaluation showing whether forecasts are calibrated and useful.
6. Multiple independent data families contributing information.
7. A user interface that explains current state, trends, forecasts, evidence, and historical performance.

## Cost strategy

Use free and low cost sources during the proof stage. Introduce paid services only when they improve reliability, coverage, latency, or model quality enough to justify recurring cost.

## Time horizon

Optimize for durable progress over months and years. A visually impressive dashboard with weak data foundations is lower priority than a modest interface backed by reliable historical data.
